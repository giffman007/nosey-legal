Common questions

My alarm did not ring. On iOS 26 and later NOSEY uses Apple's alarm system and rings even in silent mode once you allow alarms. On iOS 17 to 25 it uses notifications: keep the ringer on, volume up, and allow Time Sensitive notifications for NOSEY in Settings, Notifications.

Protect went quiet after I locked the phone. Protect keeps watching while the phone is locked or in the app switcher. If the app is force-closed while armed, a backup alarm rings within about 20 seconds in Loud mode. Silent and Ghost modes cannot ring after a force-close and log it in History instead.

I bought Pro on another phone. Settings, Restore purchase, with the same Apple Account.

How do I delete everything? Delete the app. All recordings, photos and settings are removed with it.
