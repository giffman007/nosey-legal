NOSEY Protect

Phone guard and custom alarms for iPhone.

Arm Nosey, set your phone down, and whoever picks it up gets your recorded warning, a siren, and if you turn it on, their photo. Record your own wake-up alarms, trim and remix them in the Studio, and let Nosey ring with your voice.

Everything stays on your phone. No account, no cloud, no tracking.
