Privacy policy

NOSEY Protect for iOS. Effective 1 November 2026.

The short version

NOSEY Protect does not collect, store or transmit any personal data. There is no account, no server of ours, no analytics and no advertising. Everything the app records or captures lives on your phone and is deleted when you delete it or the app.

What stays on your device

  • Audio you record or import. Used for your alarms and the Protect warning voice. Stored only in the app's private storage on your phone.
  • Photos. If you turn on Busted Cam, the front camera takes a picture when Protect is triggered. Pictures you choose from your library for the Protect screen are copied into the app. All of these stay on your phone and are never uploaded by the app. You can delete them from the Protect history at any time.
  • Motion data. Protect reads the accelerometer and gyroscope while armed to detect a pickup. The readings are used in the moment and never saved.
  • Settings and alarm times. Stored in a local database on your phone.

Face ID and passcode

NOSEY uses Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode, through Apple's system prompt, to confirm it is you before an alarm is stopped or Protect is disarmed. The app never sees your biometric data. The optional NOSEY passcode is stored only on the phone.

Purchases

NOSEY Pro is a one-time purchase handled by Apple. To confirm that purchase and restore it on a new phone, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives a random identifier for the app installation and the App Store receipt. It receives no name, email, audio, photos or other data. RevenueCat's own policy is at revenuecat.com/privacy.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Microphone, to record alarms and warnings.
  • Alarms (AlarmKit on iOS 26 and later) or notifications, so alarms can ring from the lock screen.
  • Camera, only if you turn on Busted Cam.
  • Photos, only when you pick a picture for the Protect screen.
  • Motion, while Protect is armed.

Each one can be turned off in iOS Settings. The app keeps working with whatever you allow.

Children

NOSEY Protect is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

Photos of other people

Busted Cam photographs whoever picks up your phone while Protect is armed. You are responsible for using that feature lawfully where you are. The app never shares those pictures; sharing is a manual action you take.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new date. The app itself links to this page.
